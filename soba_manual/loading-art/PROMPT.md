# Shared restaurant artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool; JPEG conversion for packaging.

Prompt: A professional widescreen hand-painted digital illustration of a welcoming small soba noodle restaurant seen from the street at a slightly elevated three-quarter viewpoint, open storefront, wooden tables, steaming bowls, warm pendant lights, red and cream awning, plants and a few pedestrians. Soft evening light; cream, forest green and terracotta. Main restaurant at middle-right, quiet dark area at left for UI. No lettering, captions, loading bars, watermark or redesigned application icon.

The same JPEG is copied into Windows, web, Android and iPhone builds. Titles, status and publisher are rendered by code.
